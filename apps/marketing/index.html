Product

Everything your agency's operations need, in one place

Every stage of a travel enquiry is handled inside a single workspace, so nothing falls through the cracks between your team's first call and the final payment.

Leads and enquiries

Capture every enquiry with contact details, travel intent and source, then move it through your pipeline with stages, priorities and team assignment.

Follow-ups and reminders

Schedule follow-ups and reminders so no enquiry goes cold. Booking reminders and a notification inbox keep the whole team on track.

Quotations and itineraries

Build professional quotations with day-by-day itineraries, hotels, sightseeing, transportation and inclusions — then share a web link or PDF.

Bookings and operations

Convert a confirmed quotation into a booking, add travellers, services, itineraries and schedules, and keep costs, documents and notes together.

Payments and customers

Track payment schedules, receipts and refunds against each booking, and keep a single customer profile that links every lead, quotation and booking.

Team and access control

Invite team members with role-based permissions and permission templates, so each person sees only what their job needs. Every change is logged.

How It Works

A clear flow from first enquiry to loyal customer

1

Capture a lead

Log the enquiry with contact and travel details. Every lead keeps a timeline of notes, follow-ups and quotations.

2

Schedule follow-ups

Set reminders so the team follows up at the right time. Nothing slips between busy days.

3

Build and share a quotation

4

Convert to a booking

Turn the confirmed quotation into a booking in one step, carrying the itinerary and traveller details forward.

5

Manage travellers and suppliers

Organise travellers, services, itineraries and vendor costs as the trip takes shape.

6

Track payments and documents

Follow payment schedules, attach documents and keep reminders — so collections and paperwork are handled on time.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Interscale Travel CRM?

A web-based CRM built for travel agencies that manages the full operational workflow — leads, follow-ups, quotations, bookings, payments and customers — in one system.

Who is it designed for?

Travel agencies and tour operators that want to move their enquiry-to-payment workflow out of spreadsheets and email threads into a single shared workspace.

Can it manage quotations and itineraries?

Yes. You can build day-by-day itineraries with hotels, sightseeing, transportation and services, add inclusions, exclusions and terms, and keep versions of each quotation.

Can quotations be shared through a web link?

Yes. Each quotation can be shared as a public weblink that clients can open, review and accept, or delivered as a downloadable PDF.

Can bookings and payments be tracked?

Yes. Convert quotations into bookings, manage travellers and services, and track payment schedules, payments and refunds — for clients and for vendors.

Can multiple team members use the CRM?

Yes. Add users, assign them to leads and bookings, and keep everyone on the same data inside one company account.

Does it support roles and permissions?

Yes. Granular permissions, roles and reusable permission templates control what each team member can view and do, and activity logs keep a record of changes.

Where do existing users log in?

Current customers sign in at the application login page: app.travelagencycrm.in/login.
